About Kuivasjärven Rantasaunat

We rent out three saunas by the evening on the shore of Kuivasjärvi in Seinäjoki: a smoke sauna, a wood-fired lakeside sauna and a small cabin sauna.

How booking works

Send a booking request with the sauna and the evening you would like. We confirm by phone.
