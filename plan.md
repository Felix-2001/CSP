# Website proposal: Kuivasjärven Rantasaunat

## 1. Goal and must-haves
- Visitors see which sauna fits and which evenings are free, without calling first
- Three saunas (smoke, wood-fired lakeside, cabin) with capacity and price, weekends cost more
- Free evenings for the next two weeks, shown with words and symbols, not only colour (colour-blind safe)
- Booking request form (sauna and evening), confirmed by phone
- Form catches mistakes and says clearly when the request is ready
- Colours: charcoal grey, off-white, lake teal

## 2. Visitors
- Locals planning an evening with friends or family: want to see what is free and what it costs
- Groups (birthdays, clubs, companies): look at capacity first, then price
- Visitors from outside the region: want to know what to expect and how to find you

## 3. Content plan
- **index.html:** welcome, photo, the three saunas in one line each. Next step: see the saunas
- **listing.html:** a card per sauna (capacity, weekday and weekend price) and the free evenings. Next step: request an evening
- **about.html:** who you are, the lake, what to expect, house rules. Next step: check availability
- **contact.html:** booking request form, phone, address. Next step: send the request

## 4. Three real sites
- **[Kangasjärvi private sauna](https://visitseinajokiregion.fi/?p=33505):** clear booking rules and contact details, but no price, capacity or free dates on the page. We use the clear rules and show the rest.
- **[Ruka smoke sauna](https://www.ruka.fi/en/node/3326):** capacity, price and what is included are stated, but free dates are hidden in an external booking app. We use the "what is included" list and show dates on the same page.
- **[Ikaalinen Spa smoke sauna](https://ikaalinenspa.fi/en/swimming-saunas/smoke-sauna):** clear price and a photo gallery, but no calendar and no capacity. We use the price sentence and the gallery.

## 5. Tone
- Calm, warm and plain, like a friendly neighbour at the lake
- Key messages: your own sauna for the evening, three saunas to choose from, see what is free and we call to confirm

## 6. What we need from you
- Capacity and weekday and weekend price per sauna (until then: marked placeholders)
- Which days count as weekend and the evening time slot (until then: Friday and Saturday, one placeholder slot)
- Booked evenings for the next two weeks (until then: an example list)
- Photos of the saunas and the lake (until then: free-licence photos)
- Phone number, address, business ID (until then: placeholders)

## 7. Next steps
- After your yes: layout and colours, then the sauna cards and free/booked display
- Then the booking form with mistake checking
- Test on phone and in greyscale, then hand over. Your IT partner connects the form to your email
